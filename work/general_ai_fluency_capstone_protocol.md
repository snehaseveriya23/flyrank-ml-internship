## 1. Concrete Protocol: Exactly How to Add the Next Case Study

### Where It Lives
- **Repository Location**: `docs/case-studies/<project-slug>.md`
- **Live Portfolio Integration**: Rendered automatically under the `/case-studies` index in the public web deployment.

### The 3-Beat Narrative Structure (Reusing Week 2 Shape)
Every future case study follows this concise, high-signal 3-beat rhythm:

1. **Beat 1 — The Problem (The Bottleneck & Stakes)**:
   - What specific operational friction or predictive failure existed?
   - Why were traditional heuristics or manual workflows insufficient?
   - What was the measurable risk / opportunity cost?
2. **Beat 2 — What I Did (AI-Augmented Architecture & Execution)**:
   - What system, pipeline, or machine learning model did I engineer?
   - How was AI leveraged responsibly (leakage-safe validation, prompt architecture, tool orchestration)?
   - What key trade-offs and design constraints were navigated?
3. **Beat 3 — What Came Of It (Quantified Impact & Deliverable)**:
   - What are the concrete benchmark results (precision, recall, latency, cost reduction)?
   - What operational decision or workflow does this deliverable now automate?
   - Where can external reviewers inspect the live demo, repo, and reproducible code?

### 5-Step Execution Workflow (From Concept to Shipped in < 30 Mins)
1. **Step 1 (Context Feed)**: Open the preserved AI Project Context; drop in the repository README, test metrics, and pipeline scripts.
2. **Step 2 (3-Beat Generation)**: Run the pre-configured *Case Study Distillation Prompt* to draft the 3-beat story in my personal voice.
3. **Step 3 (Visual Asset Generation)**: Export high-resolution benchmark charts and architecture diagrams to `docs/assets/`.
4. **Step 4 (Commit & Push)**: Save markdown to `docs/case-studies/<slug>.md` and register the route in `docs/index.html`.
5. **Step 5 (Ship & Announce)**: Trigger GitHub Pages deployment and share the live artifact link.
